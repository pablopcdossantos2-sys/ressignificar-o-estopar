How to load real historical candles into the simulator

The simulator accepts local datasets and runs entirely in the browser. The goal is to keep the project free, avoid paid API dependencies, and respect market-data licenses.

Supported instruments

Brazil

WINFUT and WDOFUT, preferably 1- or 2-minute data to support 2- and 5-minute exercises.

United States

NASDAQ and S&P 500. You may import NQ/MNQ and ES/MES series and classify them in the matching categories.

Recommended option: export from TradingView

TradingView can export the data currently loaded on a chart to CSV. Choose a symbol, load the desired history, preferably select 1m or 2m, and use the chart-data export option. Then import the CSV into the Historical Simulator.

  1. Open the instrument's Superchart.
  2. Choose 1m, 2m, or 5m.
  3. Scroll left until the desired period is included.
  4. Choose “Download chart data”.
  5. Choose the correct instrument category in the simulator and import the file.
Why is 1-minute data useful? The simulator can aggregate 1-minute candles into 2m and 5m. A 5-minute dataset cannot be faithfully converted back to 2m.

Documentation: TradingView — How to export chart data and Historical intraday data limits.

Profit / Nelogica export

The parser also recognizes typical Profit exports, including semicolon-separated files with instrument, date, time, open, high, low, close, and volume. Intraday-history availability depends on the service and license.

Reference: Nelogica — Historical data availability for CSV export.

Why not download automatically from B3?

B3's official portal provides historical data and bulletins, but its trading-session search page states that the data is owned by B3 and prohibits automated collection. Publicly described intraday products also use licensing models and do not provide an open 2- or 5-minute candle dataset for redistribution. This project will not scrape B3 or copy third-party datasets without an explicit license. The simulator uses files the user is entitled to use.

References: B3 — Trading-session search and B3 DataWise — Available data.

Recognized formats

  • TradingView CSV: columns such as time, open, high, low, close, volume.
  • Profit/Nelogica CSV: columns such as Data;Hora;Abertura;Máxima;Mínima;Fechamento;Volume, including decimal commas.

Check timestamps, timezone, adjustments, continuous contracts, and rollover rules. The simulator uses imported candles as-is and does not automatically correct futures-contract continuity.

Privacy and storage

Candles are stored in the browser's IndexedDB. The project does not upload them to a server. Clearing site or browser data may remove imported datasets.
